## 1. Course Intention & Context

This intermediate mathematics course focuses on the analysis of multidimensional large databases. In the process, students identify the characteristics of different problems that can be addressed using multivariate statistical analysis techniques.

- **Required Prior Knowledge:** Probability and statistics, probability distribution functions, normal distribution, and inferential statistics (hypothesis testing and confidence intervals).
- **Primary Learning Outcome:** Students analyze a multidimensional phenomenon or process by applying one or more multivariate statistical techniques learned in the course.

---

## 2. Course Objectives & Competencies

Upon completion of this educational unit, students will be able to:

1. **Build structured, analyzable information-based databases** within an organization.
2. **Extract relevant information from large structured databases** using statistical methods and appropriate technological tools.
3. **Formulate mathematical models** most frequently used in large-scale optimization processes.
4. **Analyze the world as a set of interconnected systems**, maintaining an integrated vision in understanding and solving field-related problems.
5. **Solve problems from diverse environments of life** with ethical awareness, responsibility, and fairness.

## 3. Detailed Course Topics & Subtopics

### Topic 1: Regression Analysis
- **1.1** Simple linear regression
- **1.2** ANOVA and confidence intervals for predictions
- **1.3** Residual analysis and condition of normality
- **1.4** Forward selection
- **1.5** Backward selection
- **1.6** Non-linear regression
- **1.7** Multiple linear regression
- **1.8** Aberrant data and heteroskedasticity problems

### Topic 2: Multivariate Analysis
- **2.1** Multivariate distributions
- **2.2** Mean vectors and variance and covariance matrices
- **2.3** Correlations and correlation matrices
- **2.4** Multivariate normal probability density function
- **2.5** Lost, null, incorrect values and detection of discrepancies
- **2.6** Multivariate aberrant data
- **2.7** Sample correlations, Fisher and Ruben intervals
- **2.8** Multivariate descriptive analytics and visualization

### Topic 3: Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
- **3.1** Cases where PCA is used
- **3.2** Geometrical description and classification of major components
- **3.3** Estimation of major components
- **3.4** Determination of the appropriate number of major components
- **3.5** Coding and commercial programs

### Topic 4: Factor Analysis
- **4.1** Objectives of factor analysis
- **4.2** Factor analysis equations
- **4.3** Choice of the appropriate number of factors
- **4.4** Factor rotation
- **4.5** Oblique rotation method
- **4.6** Coding and commercial programs

### Topic 5: Discriminant Analysis
- **5.1** Discrimination for two normal multivariate populations
- **5.2** Function of costs and a priori probabilities
- **5.3** Basic discrimination
- **5.4** Stepwise selection
- **5.5** Canonical discriminant functions
- **5.6** Coding and commercial programs

### Topic 6: Analysis by Conglomerates (Cluster Analysis)
- **6.1** Similarity and dissimilarity measures
- **6.2** Graphical methods (dispersion, main components, Andrews)
- **6.3** Non-hierarchical grouping methods
- **6.4** Hierarchical grouping
- **6.5** Nearest neighbor method
- **6.6** Coding and commercial programs

### Topic 7: Multivariate Regression
- **7.1** Logistic regression model
- **7.2** Inferences for variances and covariances matrices
- **7.3** Inferences for a vector of means
- **7.4** MANOVA (Multivariate Analysis of Variance)
- **7.5** Canonical correlation analysis
- **7.6** Analysis by factors and regression
- **7.7** Programming and commercial systems

---

## 4. Specific Learning Objectives by Topic

1. **Perform regression analysis** on single and multi-variable systems.
2. **Apply multivariate analysis** techniques and diagnostics.
3. **Implement Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** for dimensionality reduction.
4. **Apply Discriminant Analysis** for supervised group separation and classification.
5. **Implement clustering methods** for unsupervised segmentation.
6. **Build multivariate regression models** and hypothesis tests (MANOVA, Hotelling's $T^2$, Canonical Correlation).

## 5. Suggested Methodologies & Learning Techniques

### Supervised Learning Activities (Led by Academic):
1. **Review and conceptual analysis** conducted by the teacher through learning modules addressing multivariate statistical modeling.
2. **Discussions and resolution** of situations related to the course challenge.
3. **Mentoring and supervision** of field work carried out by students in real environments.
4. **Individual and group counseling** sessions supporting the challenge resolution process.

### Independent Learning Activities (Individual / Collaborative):
1. **Individual and team research** on regression and multivariate statistical methods.
2. **Problem and case solving**, developing the ability to classify, group, or model data.
3. **Field work** addressing data acquisition, storage, and transformation focused on decision making.
4. **Evidence generation:** Complete final deliverable report with justification of chosen statistical methods.
